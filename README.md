# Mage Knight Web

**English** · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [日本語](README.ja.md)

A solo browser game in the style of Mage Knight, with the rules enforced automatically. Explore a hex map, combine cards in your hand into movement, influence, attack and block, race against the day/night cycle and mana, and work toward scenario goals and boss fights.

> This is a personal hobby project and is not affiliated with WizKids or the rights holders of the original board game. Cards and map tiles use placeholder art, and no official artwork is included.

Map and card icons are from [Game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/) by Lorc, Delapouite, sbed and Willdabeast, used under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Copies in `apps/web/public/game-icons/` have their backgrounds removed and are displayed in game colors.

## Features

- **Tutorial**: a fixed introductory scenario to learn the basics
- **Solo Conquest**: the four base-game heroes plus Braevalar, with the full card pool, offers, unit recruitment, skills and artifacts
- **Expansions**: the base game, Lost Legion, Krang and Shades of Tezla can be enabled on their own or combined
- **Saves and replays**: save locally in the browser, undo moves, and replay finished games
- **Works on any screen**: designed for desktop, and also playable on tablets and phones
